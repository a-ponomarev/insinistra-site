## Visitor statistics (GoatCounter)

We count page visits with [GoatCounter](https://www.goatcounter.com/). It does not use cookies and does not store personal data; we only see aggregated numbers (pages, referring sites, countries, device types).

## Meta Pixel (only with your consent)

If you click **Accept** in the cookie banner, the site loads the Meta Pixel by Meta Platforms Ireland Limited. It sets cookies (`_fbp`, up to 90 days) and sends Meta information about your visit: pages you open and clicks on ticket, Facebook event, music, social and e-mail links. We use it to measure our ads and to show our ads to people who visited the site. Meta processes this data under its own [Privacy Policy](https://facebook.com/privacy/policy/).

Legal basis: your consent (Art. 6(1)(a) GDPR). If you click **Decline**, the Meta Pixel is not loaded and nothing is sent to Meta. You can change your choice at any time via **Cookie settings** at the bottom of every page.

## Your rights

You can ask us for access to, correction or deletion of your data at [info@insinistra.com](mailto:info@insinistra.com). You also have the right to lodge a complaint with a data protection supervisory authority.
